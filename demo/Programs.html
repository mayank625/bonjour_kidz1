Play Group

Children at Play group start exploring and interacting socially. This is the time they need to be given exposure to sensory materials and activities for their wholistic development.

At Bonjour Kidz Play School & Day Care, we understand that young children grow and learn the most when they receive undivided attention and the right stimulation. As parents, you can be assured that our inclusive, engaging and interactive learning environments are capable of adapting to your child’s specific learning and development requirements.

"Early experiences in the first few years of life are critical for acquisition of skills as maximum brain development happens during this period."
Age Group: 2.5 - 3.5 YEARS
Duration: 2.5 Hours Per Day

Pre Nursery

Nursery

Quality education in the early years of preschool sets a firm foundation in the overall development of a child. At Bonjour Kidz Play School & Day Care, the nursery curriculum provides a right array of activities to help children move towards achieving their early learning goals.

In Nursery, children are engaged in school readiness activities such as reading, writing, counting, number recognition and problem solving in a fun filled but purposeful manner. Children are encouraged to corroborate their learning through collaborative activities in the areas of dramatics, science and arts.

"Early experiences in the first few years of life are critical for acquisition of skills as maximum brain development happens during this period."
Age Group: 2.5 - 3.5 YEARS
Duration: 2.5 Hours Per Day

KINDERGARTEN(K.G)

The transition from preschool to primary school is a crucial time of change for both children as well as their parents. At Bonjour Kidz Play School & Day Care, the Kindergarten (K.G.) curriculum ensures smooth transition to formal schooling with meaningful and progressive learning experiences.

In addition to age-appropriate activities in Kindergarten, which prepare a child for success in formal school; various other activities are conducted in the areas of language, numbers, general knowledge, music, art in a planned manner throughout the program. Through these activities, children learn the alphabet and letter sounds, numbers and simple math and master early writing skills.

Day Care

Bonjour Kidz Play School & Daycare program includes preschool and post that a nap time, meals and evening activities. The Day care program has been carefully designed for children from the age of 9 Months+ such that the child is constructively engaged and involved in different explorations before returning to their parents.
