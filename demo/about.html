About Bonjour PLay School

At Bonjour Play School & Day Care, children are nurtured in an atmosphere of love & affection which alone facilities the harmonious development of young minds. We emphasize on letting the child learn by playing and exploring.


Bonjour Play School & Day Care help your child be a winner for life:“HELP ME DO IT MY SELF” is the life theme of our school. The children are encouraged to learn. How to learn and ‘I can do it’ attitude. Our aim is to help young minds grow and learn in an amicable environment, resulting in the all-round development of the child. We want to play responsible and positive role in shaping up your child’s tomorrow.
